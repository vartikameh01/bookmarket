Dear Students,

All students of B.Tech. CSE Final Year are instructed to initiate and regularly monitor the progress of their Major Projects under the guidance of their respective supervisors. You must adhere to the following instructions and timelines:

Topic Approval: Meet your respective supervisor on or before 24th October 2026 to discuss and obtain approval for your major project topic.

Submission of Documents: Submit the Project Approval Form and Project Synopsis (attached with this mail) to your supervisor on or before 31st October 2026.

Weekly Progress Meetings: Meet your supervisor every week to present your project progress, discuss challenges, receive feedback, and incorporate the suggested improvements into your project.

Progress Evaluation: Your supervisor will evaluate your project progress every 15 days. You are expected to demonstrate continuous progress and complete the assigned tasks within the specified timeline.

Project Development: At least 20% of the project coding and implementation must be completed by 30th November 2026.

Final Project Presentation: The Final Major Project Presentation will be held on 7th December 2026. Students must ensure that their projects are adequately developed and that they are prepared to demonstrate their progress and explain their project work during the presentation.

Project Type and Quality: Your project may be application-based or research-based, as decided in consultation with your supervisor. Ensure that your work reflects technical competence, innovation, and progress towards the defined objectives.

All students are advised to take these instructions seriously and maintain regular coordination with their supervisors. Failure to obtain topic approval or submit the required documents by 31st October 2026 may affect the subsequent project evaluation process.

Note: Early Joinee students will appear in the Final Major Project Presentation.

Please plan your project activities carefully and ensure that all milestones are achieved within the prescribed timelines.
